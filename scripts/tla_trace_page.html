Majority lease counterexamples

Each of these is a sequence of events that the model checker TLC found in MajorityLeaseHA.tla, the specification of the proposed leadership design. Six of them come from runs with one rule removed or changed, which shows that the rule is needed. One comes from the design as proposed, and shows that an epoch can go backwards.

There are three voters: instance 1, instance 2 and the arbiter. (When the same design is applied to the two arbiters, the third voter is the witness.) An instance acts as leader only while it holds two of the three votes: its own, plus an unexpired lease from one of the others. Step through with the buttons or the arrow keys. Values that changed at a step are highlighted.

request for a vote grant refusal acting as leader
A tick is one unit of the model's clock, and the lease period is __LEASE_TICKS__ ticks. A message can take any number of ticks to arrive, or be lost. Epochs record which instance leads in them: an epoch whose remainder on division by four is 1 is led by instance 1, and one whose remainder is 2 by instance 2. The full results are in docs/availability/tla/findings.md, section 11, and the design is in docs/availability/majority_leases.md.
