# Release v0.4.0 {#release_v0_4_0}

Released 2026-09-14. 217 commits since v0.3.0; 375 files changed.

A matching engine that keeps its open orders across a restart, leadership that survives
components restarting, a venue that checks a member's sequence numbers and refuses orders it
cannot process, and a functional specification whose build checks what it claims.

Development is paused after this release. [Project status](../../project_status.md) says what
works, what is known to be wrong, and where to start.

`CHANGELOG.md`, in the repository root, carries the full list. Not linked from here: it is not
one of doxygen's inputs, so a link to it cannot be resolved and warnings are fatal.

## Highlights

### A matching engine keeps its open orders across a restart

The book is held in a memory-mapped region of fixed-size records that outlives the process. A
restarted engine reads its orders back, asks the sequencer for everything after the position the
region records, checks it received exactly as many records as were sent, and only then acts. A
thousand orders were recovered in 56 ms, and every cancel the member then sent was accepted.

Where the venue could not match for longer than configuration allows while orders were open, the
engine cancels them, tells each member, and halts: those orders are priced for a market that has
moved while their owners could not withdraw them.

### Leadership survives components restarting

Scenarios 24 to 39 restart each component of each high availability pair, in each role, with and
without a launcher. Writing them found that almost every leadership decision assumed "primary"
and "leader" meant the same instance. A restarted primary took leadership without asking; orders
went to the instance named primary rather than the leader; replication ran one way, so the pair
survived one failure and not two; an arbiter forgot who led; and epochs went back to zero. All of
those are fixed.

`scripts/launch.py` restarts the one process it starts, and `devenv.py --supervised` uses it. An
instance that interrupts the venue twice is not restarted again.

### The venue checks the sequence numbers a member sends

Before this, an order numbered 50 when the venue expected 3 was accepted, and 47 missing messages
went unnoticed on both sides. A gap is now answered with a ResendRequest, repeated twice at
five-second intervals before the session ends; a low number is discarded if marked as a possible
duplicate and ends the session if not.

### Orders the venue cannot process are refused

When no matching engine has been able to process orders for too long, the sequencer tells every
gateway, and the gateways answer new orders and cancels with a rejection and a reason. Before
this, a member's orders were acknowledged and then never answered.

### The sequencer no longer stalls on its own log

The thread every order passes through waited up to 557 ms for the ext4 journal to allocate
blocks for a new log segment. The next segment is now created and filled ahead of the writer. The
filesystem holding the log must be mounted `lazytime`; see
[filesystem requirements](../../operations/filesystem_requirements.md).

### A functional specification

`docs/book` states 139 requirements with permanent identifiers, each giving its reason. Its build
fails on a requirement with no reason, a scenario that does not exist, or a gap citing a defect
the bug list records as closed. 24 requirements are verified by a scenario so far.

## Upgrading from 0.3.0

- Every script is in `scripts/`.
- `[matching_engine] ha_enabled` is gone; `[ha] enabled` is the single switch.
- `order_book.open_orders_on_promotion` is required in every matching engine configuration.
- The filesystem holding the sequencer's log must be mounted `lazytime`.
- cpptrace is a new third-party dependency.
- The gateway pool metric `pool_chain_length` is now `pool_segments`.
- Several PDUs changed, including `LeadershipLease` and the new `OrderAcceptance`. There is no
  compatibility between 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 components on the wire.

## Known limitations

- 33 bugs are open, 12 of them high severity. See the [bug list](../../bug_list.md).
- The write-ahead log retains everything and nothing reclaims it
  ([BUG-0048](../../bug_list.md#bug_0048)).
- 115 of the 139 requirements have no scenario checking them
  ([BUG-0068](../../bug_list.md#bug_0068)).
- The Rocky image shares RHEL8's gcc but not its Python, so pylint, pytest and the pybind11
  extension build still need checking on the real target.
