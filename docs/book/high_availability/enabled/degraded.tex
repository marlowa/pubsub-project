\subsection{Running degraded}
\label{sec:ha:enabled:degraded}

Every failure in this section leaves the venue in a state, and for several of
them that state is: working, and no longer able to survive the next failure.

A group whose second instance has not yet been restarted is serving from one.
A venue that has lost both arbiters is trading, and cannot move an entitlement if
it needs to. Neither condition stops anything, neither produces an error, and
from outside neither is distinguishable from a venue in full health.

\textbf{The venue must run in these states rather than refuse to.} Losing
redundancy is not a reason to stop: that would turn the loss of a spare into the
outage the spare existed to prevent, and it would mean the venue was less
available for having been made highly available. But running degraded silently is
not acceptable either, because the exposure is real and bounded only by how long
it lasts.

\subsubsection{What the venue must do}
\label{sec:ha:enabled:degraded:behaviour}

\begin{requirement}{R-0093}{The venue trades while a group is reduced to one instance}
  The loss of one instance of a group shall not stop members trading, and shall
  not stop the surviving instance acting.
  \rationale{A pair reduced to one has lost its protection against the next
  failure and has lost nothing else. Refusing to work in that state causes an
  outage now rather than risking one later.}
  \covers{ha_test:3, ha_test:6}
\end{requirement}

\begin{requirement}{R-0094}{A group running without its second instance is knowable}
  The venue shall make it possible to establish, at any moment, which groups are
  running with a second instance and which are not.
  \rationale{The window of no resilience lasts from the failure until something
  restores the missing instance, and its length is the venue's exposure to a
  second failure. Automatic restart makes it short and also makes it invisible ---
  a component that dies and stays dead is eventually noticed by somebody
  downstream, and one that is restarted repeatedly is noticed by nobody. What is
  automated has to be reported, or nothing is watching the thing that used to
  announce itself by breaking.}
  \uncovered{no scenario asks whether a group is running single, and nothing answers}
\end{requirement}

\begin{requirement}{R-0095}{An instance may act with no arbiter reachable, and says that it did}
  Where no arbiter can be reached and an entitlement must be settled, an instance
  may take it by the rule that orders a cold start, and shall record that it did
  so without arbitration.
  \rationale{Refusing to act because the party that arbitrates is unreachable
  stops the venue for the failure of a component that holds no venue state and is
  off the order path --- which is the opposite of what it is for. This is
  nonetheless the one moment the venue is not protected against a second
  claimant, so it is also the one that must not pass unrecorded.}
  \covers{ha_test:8, ha_test:9, ha_test:35, ha_test:39}
\end{requirement}
