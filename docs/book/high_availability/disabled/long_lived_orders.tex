\subsection{Orders that outlive the trading day}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:long_lived}

Most orders are for the session that placed them or for the day. Some are not: an
order may be placed to rest for weeks, and its member expects to find it there
each morning without doing anything.

Everything else in this section concerns state that must survive a process, or a
machine. These orders must survive \emph{the venue} --- every process stopped
deliberately, the day ended, the machines rebooted, and trading begun again the
next morning.

\subsubsection{Why the mechanisms already described do not serve}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:long_lived:why}

A checkpoint records the orders held at a point in the sequence, and is useful
only while the record it is anchored to still holds what followed that point
(\cref{sec:ha:disabled:me:durable}). That relationship is what a day boundary
breaks: a new day's record does not continue the previous day's, and retaining
every day's record so that a checkpoint from any of them remains usable is not a
retention policy anybody would choose.

So an order intended to outlive the day needs a durable home of its own, held
independently of the record of what the venue did, and read when trading begins
rather than recovered when something fails.

\textbf{No component reaches that home while it is trading.} The venue's
components hold no connection to the store, in the same way and for the same
reason that admission does not depend on one: a store that must be reachable for
the venue to work is a store whose every outage is a trading outage. The orders
are exported and read when trading begins, and the store is brought up to date
from the venue's own record of what it did --- which is behind the venue by
design, and can be brought level again from that record because the record is
what it is derived from.

That has one consequence for the requirement below. What must be durable before a
member is told its order has been cancelled is \emph{the venue's own record},
not the store. The store follows.

\subsubsection{What the venue must do}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:long_lived:behaviour}

\begin{requirement}{R-0105}{An order that outlives the trading day is held where it survives the venue stopping}
  An order whose terms say it remains open beyond the trading day shall be
  recorded where it survives every component stopping and the day ending.
  \rationale{The member was told the order would rest until it said otherwise.
  Holding it only where the day's own state is held makes that promise good for
  one day, which is not the promise that was made.}
  \uncovered{such orders are held only for the life of the venue's processes}
\end{requirement}

\begin{requirement}{R-0106}{Such orders are open when trading next begins}
  When trading begins, every order that was open at the end of the previous day
  and has not since expired shall be open, on the terms it was placed under, and
  cancellable by the member that placed it.
  \rationale{A member that must re-place its resting orders each morning has not
  been given orders that outlive the day; it has been given orders that are
  quietly cancelled overnight, and told otherwise.}
  \uncovered{the venue has no notion of a trading day}
\end{requirement}

\begin{requirement}{R-0114}{The store is known to be readable before trading begins, not during it}
  Whether the store holding these orders can be read shall be established before
  trading starts, and its being unreadable shall stop trading from starting
  rather than be discovered once it has.
  \rationale{These orders are read once, at the start of a day. A store that
  cannot be read then produces a venue that opens with an empty set and no
  indication that anything is missing --- and every member holding such an order
  finds it gone, at the one moment nobody is watching for it. The check is cheap
  and the moment to make it is before members are admitted.}
  \uncovered{the store holds no orders and nothing reads it}
\end{requirement}

\begin{requirement}{R-0107}{A change to such an order is committed before the member is told of it}
  Where such an order is cancelled, amended or filled, that shall be committed to
  the venue's own record before the member is told, and the store the order
  outlives the day in shall be brought up to date from that record.
  \rationale{A member told its order is cancelled stops watching for it. If
  nothing durable is written first, a failure between the two brings the order
  back the next time the store is read --- live, on a market that has moved, for a
  member with no reason to look. Requiring the venue's own record rather than the
  store is what allows the store to lag: anything it is missing can be supplied
  again from the record, where a change that was never committed anywhere cannot
  be.}
  \uncovered{such orders are not recorded durably at all}
\end{requirement}

\begin{gap}{}
  Orders that outlive the trading day are held only where every other open order
  is held, which does not survive the venue stopping. A member placing one is
  given an order that lasts until the next time the venue is restarted, and is
  told nothing to that effect.
\end{gap}
