\subsection{The gateway together with another component}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:gw_and_others}

The gateway depends on the sequencer for everything it recovers, and on the
matching engine for everything it asks to be done on a member's behalf. Losing
either alongside it is therefore a distinct case, and stating it is worth doing
even where the requirements are ones already made: it says which combinations
the venue is designed to survive rather than leaving a reader to infer it.

\subsubsection{The gateway and the sequencer}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:gw_and_seq}

A member reconnects to a restarted gateway, which asks a sequencer that has
itself just restarted and no longer holds the session. Nothing new is required:
\reqref{R-0011} says the numbering must survive, and \reqref{R-0012} says that
where it has not, the venue says so rather than resuming the member at the
beginning. What the combination adds is that this is the likeliest way for the
second of those to be reached, because a session that was not bound at the
moment the sequencer restarted is one nobody is reporting.

\subsubsection{The gateway and the matching engine}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:gw_and_me}

A member's connection ends while the matching engine is not running. The gateway
does not act on the member's orders itself; it reports the disconnection and the
terms it was given to the component that holds them --- and that component is
absent.

\begin{requirement}{R-0037}{A member's cancel-on-disconnect instruction is not lost because a component was restarting}
  Where a session ends while the component that holds its orders is unavailable,
  the instruction shall be acted on once that component returns, or the member
  shall be told that it was not.
  \rationale{The member configured this to bound its exposure when its own
  connection fails. An instruction dropped because a venue process happened to
  be restarting leaves it exposed for a reason it has no way to discover, and
  the venue has no way to discover either.}
  \uncovered{no scenario ends a session while the engine is down}
\end{requirement}
