\subsection{Stopping and starting deliberately}
\label{sec:ha:disabled:stopping}

Not every stop is a failure. A component is taken down for a release, for a
configuration change, or because the trading day has ended, and none of those
should cost what a crash costs.

\begin{requirement}{R-0034}{An orderly stop leaves less to recover than a crash}
  A component asked to stop shall record its state before it goes, so that what
  it has to recover afterwards is no more than what it wrote.
  \rationale{A planned restart is the common one --- a release, a configuration
  change --- and there is no reason for it to cost what an unplanned one does.
  It is also the cheapest way to exercise the recovery path regularly.}
  \uncovered{a component records nothing when it is asked to stop}
\end{requirement}

\begin{requirement}{R-0035}{The start and end of a trading day are published}
  The venue shall publish the start of a trading day and its end as events that
  any interested party may subscribe to.
  \rationale{Whether a venue that has just started is resuming a trading day or
  beginning a new one changes what every recovery in this section means, and it
  should be answerable rather than inferred. Publishing it once to whoever
  subscribes also avoids each component being told separately, which is how they
  come to disagree.}
  \uncovered{the venue has no notion of a trading day}
\end{requirement}
